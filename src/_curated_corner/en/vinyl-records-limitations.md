Some interesting limitations of vinyl records:

1. [The higher the volume, the shorter the recording time][1]
2. [The more bass, the shorter the recording time][1]
3. [Achieving both high and loud trebles is not possible][2]
4. [Too much stereo effects is not possible][3]
