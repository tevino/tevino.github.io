I've been immersed in AI due to its accelerating development. Models are becoming more capable, even the small ones. Agents are becoming better at complex and diverse workloads. I can build a working prototype from an idea in a day, which might have taken me days, weeks, or even months of work previously. And there's OpenClaw and all kinds of "claws" appearing. It's exciting but also overwhelming.

But going offline, little has changed. People are still doing things the old way. Some still think AI can't help them even in simple tasks like consolidating a report from multiple sources. To me, these things make the AI progress online feel like another world, like a dream. Going offline is like seeing the harsh white light when walking out of a movie theater.

Some think the AI boom is a bubble like the dot-com era. I think the progress is real. We're just in the lag period until adoption catches up.
