As a learner of pixel art, I love using Procreate on my iPad with Apple Pencil.

There are other apps specifically designed for creating pixel art, but Procreate's smooth experience and gestures are just joyful to use.

## The problem of Procreate export

The issue comes when I want to export my pixel art. Procreate's export feature is great for high-resolution images, but it's not ideal for pixel art; the result is a blurry image.

The only solution in Procreate is to tediously apply some changes to the canvas and layersjust for this export; that means if you want to keep your artwork intact, you have to undo those changes after the export, and these back-and-forth steps are recorded into the timelapse video.

I don't think this is a good experience for pixel artists.

## Introducing Pixel Companion

So I built [Pixel Companion][pc] for iPhone and iPad, now [on the App Store][pc_appstore].

It enables lossless upscaling, optimized specifically for pixel art to prevent blurring and artifacts; you can share your work directly to [Pixel Companion][pc] without even opening it.

The app also helps explore diverse color palettes online.

I made [Pixel Companion][pc] to solve my own scaling issues as a pixel art learner. I hope it can assist other artists like you to better create pixel art.

I'm open to feedback to improve the app's usefulness. Please [let me know][email] how [Pixel Companion][pc] could better meet your needs!!
