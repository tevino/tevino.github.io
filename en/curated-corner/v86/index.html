v86

EN | ZH

v86 is a collection of operating systems that you can run inside your browser by emulating an x86 platform, there are Linux, BSD, Windows, DOS, Unix-like systems, and others that don’t belong to any.

If you’re looking for a Macintosh or NeXT system, try Infinite Mac.
